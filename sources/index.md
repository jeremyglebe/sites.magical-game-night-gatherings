# Sources and Reference Notes

These pages preserve research, attribution, and historical context. To play, choose a version under [Formats](../formats/) and read its rules page. Source notes can describe different or optional rules; they are not a shared ruleset.

For saved card-pool research, use [Project Research](../research/): [Marvel sets and supporting pools](../research/marvel-card-pools.md) and the [everyday-life card catalogue](../research/everyday-life.md), including its separately identified almost matches.

## Official formats and local rules

[Archenemy](../formats/archenemy/), [Cube](../formats/cube/), and [Jumpstart](../formats/jumpstart/) link to Wizards references on their rules pages. [Pile o' Magic](../formats/big-deck/), [Hitmen](../formats/assassin/), and [Creature Feature](../formats/creature-feature/) are local rules recorded in this repository.

Jumpstart's [custom-packet preparation guide](../formats/jumpstart/building-packets.md) records this catalogue's design preferences and protected sleeve-label method. Those are local preparation conventions, distinct from the official packet-based play structure.
