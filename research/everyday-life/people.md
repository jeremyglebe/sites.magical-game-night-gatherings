# People and Ordinary Jobs Beyond Marvel

Reporters, delivery workers, medical staff, shopkeepers, investigators, and other ordinary people from the rest of Magic. Checked October 19, 2026. Exact type lines matter: a believable human occupation is not automatically the Citizen creature type.

**Direct** entries have a strong everyday-life premise. **Setting** entries keep an ordinary role but carry a fantasy, historical, or other-franchise setting. Werewolves, fantasy-species civilians, and other qualified exceptions are listed separately under [Almost matches](almost-matches.md#people-and-jobs).
