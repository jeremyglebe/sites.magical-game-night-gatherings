# Ordinary-Animal Tokens

**Checked October 19, 2026.** There really are Magic tokens named simply **Cat**, **Dog**, **Rat**, **Bird**, **Mouse**, **Squirrel**, and **Bear**. The selected examples below have straightforward animal profiles. A token name does not uniquely specify its colors, size, or abilities.

Other versions differ. Cats can have lifelink, haste, or even flying; Dogs can be 3/1 with vigilance; Rats can have deathtouch, lifelink, a can't-block restriction, or a scaling bonus; Birds have several sizes and combat restrictions. Use the token specified by the effect creating it, or the exact profile selected by the box's written rules. Do not substitute a different profile merely because the name matches.

These are token components, not ordinary cards to shuffle into a normal deck. A [Horde](../../formats/horde/) may use physical token cards in its library according to its own selected ruleset. That is a format rule, not a general permission to construct a normal Cat deck from token cards.

For regular creature cards, use the [pet and wildlife catalogue](animals.md) and [Marvel animals](marvel.md#animals). The complete names and printing links are saved there, including City Pigeon, Lucky the Pizza Dog, Sanctuary Cat, and many others.
