# Useful Products & Tools

Saved products and tools for assembling, storing, and reusing game-night boxes. Keep the exact size and product identifier when reordering: stock, prices, and marketplace listings can change. **Last reviewed: October 21, 2026.**

For cube drafting, [Dragon Shield Cube Shells](#dragon-shield-cube-shells) and [decorative Cubeamajigs](#cubeamajigs-decorative-booster-packs) provide reusable booster containers. For the twenty-four-packet Jumpstart project, Burger Tokens is the preferred box family. The [storage research](jumpstart-storage.md) records the fit assumptions and comparisons; use the [card storage calculator](card-storage-calculator.md) with the actual sleeves and inserts before choosing a size.

## Storage boxes

### Dragon Shield Cube Shells

**Reusable booster containers for cube drafts.** Dragon Shield specifies **eight shells per retail pack**, each holding **fifteen double-sleeved cards or twenty single-sleeved cards**. They have a secure closure and a writing field on top for labeling. These are the manufacturer's capacity claims, not measurements of this project's sleeves. [Manufacturer's product page](https://www.dragonshield.com/products/cube-shell-blood-red)

- **Reorder identifiers:** ASIN **`B09W6CQPHH`**; model **`AT-30550`**; **Dragon Shield Cube Shell — Blood Red**.
- **Draft quantity:** an eight-player [booster-draft cube](../formats/cube/variants/booster-draft.md), using three fifteen-card packs per player, needs **twenty-four shells: three retail eight-packs**.

**Find it again:** use the manufacturer's product page or [Dragon Shield's Shells collection](https://www.dragonshield.com/collections/shells), or search **Dragon Shield Cube Shell Blood Red AT-30550**. Match the Cube Shell name, color, and eight-pack quantity when reordering.

## Tools

### The Sturdy Deck Press

[MakerWorld model and print files](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1884468-the-sturdy-deck-press) by [Nabes Teban](https://makerworld.com/en/@NabesTeban). A **3D-printable press for removing trapped air from sleeved or double-sleeved cards**, suggested for the Jumpstart preparation kit. Cards load from the side; the upper tray attaches to the screw, and an optional lower tray helps with removal.

- **Print profile:** the designer's [profile `2018145`](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1884468-the-sturdy-deck-press#profileId-2018145) specifies **0.2 mm layers, nine walls, and 33% infill**. The original design uses **ABS**; the designer also recommends **PETG** in the comments. Check the current profile and assembly instructions for the chosen printer and material.
- **Small-packet fit:** the designer confirms that the screw intentionally stops short of the base and suggests adding tokens, lands, or bulk cards when a stack is too short. An individual twenty- or twenty-five-card Jumpstart packet may need that additional stack height. Pressing does not establish a fixed final card thickness; measure the actual packet for the [storage calculator](card-storage-calculator.md).
- **Find it again:** MakerWorld model **`1884468`**, **The STURDY DECK PRESS**, creator **Nabes Teban**. The creator's profile provides a fallback if the model URL changes.
