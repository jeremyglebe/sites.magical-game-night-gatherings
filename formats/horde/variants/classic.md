# Classic Zombie Horde

[Horde versions](../) · **Rules: Peter Knudson's September 2011 version**

## Players and materials

One to four survivors cooperate against an automated Zombie deck. Each survivor uses a Commander deck; the original source also permits other Magic decks by agreement.

Build a 100-card Horde from approximately 60 token cards and 40 ordinary cards. Token cards are physically shuffled into the Horde library and are cast by the special procedure below. Choose cards that work without a human pilot. Use the [original Zombie decklist](../sources/original-horde-magic-quiet-speculation.md#original-zombie-horde-deck) if you want the published list.

## Setup

| Survivors | Shared life | Cards used from the shuffled Horde | Survivor setup turns |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 20 | 45 | 3 |
| 2 | 40 | 60 | 3 |
| 3 | 60 | 75 | 3 |
| 4 | 80 | 100 | 3 |

1. Shuffle the 100-card Horde and randomly set aside enough cards to leave the number in the table.
2. The Horde begins with no cards in hand and no permanents.
3. Survivors shuffle their decks, prepare any commanders, draw their opening hands, and use normal mulligans.
4. Set the survivor team's shared life total to 20 per player.
5. Survivors take **three shared turns** before the Horde takes its first turn.

## Survivor turns and resources

Survivors take their phases and steps together. Each player draws from their own library, controls their own permanents, uses their own mana, and makes their own land play. They coordinate attacks and blocks.

Damage, life loss, and life gain affecting the survivors change their shared life total. The source's special shared-life setup replaces the usual individual Commander starting life.

## Damaging and defeating the Horde

The Horde has no life total. Each point of damage dealt to it puts one card from the top of its library into its graveyard. Survivors may attack it to reduce future waves.

The survivors win when **the Horde library is empty and no Horde Zombies remain on the battlefield**. An empty library alone is not victory while Zombies remain.

The Horde wins when the survivor team's shared life reaches **zero or less**.

## Source limitations and other versions

The original article does not specify a complete set of special rules for poison, alternate-win cards, or every modern card type. It also does not have the follow-up's planeswalker attack procedure. Record additional decisions before using interactions that need them.

For a specific planeswalker procedure and the 2011 ban list, choose the [Open Source version](open-source.md). For a different life system and rarity-based waves, choose [HordeMagic.com](hordemagic.md).
