# Pile o’ Magic

[Big Deck versions](../) · **Local rules: shared-library game**

Pile o' Magic is the owner's formalization of Big Deck Magic. The complete recorded rules follow. For the Commander version, use [Pile o' Magic Commander](commander.md).

## Setup

Prepare a Messy or Themed Pile using the construction rules below, plus the agreed Heap Lands or land-area markers. Use normal multiplayer setup, starting at 20 life and drawing seven cards unless the table chooses another underlying format. Draw from the shared Pile. Normal individual turns and normal Magic victory conditions apply unless changed below.

## Copy limits: Singlesome

Part of the fun of Pile o’ Magic is the element of discovery– and of unique games. In general, you should try to avoid including multiple copies of cards.

Of course, when making a *Messy Pile*\*, you could end up with duplicates even if players avoid bringing them. Additionally, some cards only make sense to include with duplicates (**S.N.O.T.**)

The maximum copies of a single card allowed in the Pile is 4x(number of Players). Except for the exceptions, of course (**Rat Colony**, **Hare Apparent**).

## Local rules still needing decisions

The recorded design leaves these interactions for a future ruling:

- Who owns a card drawn from the shared library for in-game ownership effects, and what happens when a contributor leaves the game.
- The order of simultaneous draws from the Pile and handling of mulligans involving the same library.
- Whether exchanging a card for a Heap Land is a special action or an ability, and its exact timing within upkeep.
- The chosen four-color Heap Land option.

The standard last-player-standing and ordinary Magic loss conditions apply unless the group explicitly chooses otherwise. Any new box must state its answers to the shared-library questions rather than assume another Big Deck format's rules.
