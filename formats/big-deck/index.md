# Big Deck

[All formats](../)

Big Deck formats use a shared library or communal card pool. Different groups resolve shared-library and land rules differently; choose the local version you intend to play.

| Ruleset | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| [Pile o' Magic](pile-o-magic/) | The complete recorded shared-library rules, Heap Lands, Pile construction, copy limits, and card restrictions. |
| [Pile o' Magic Commander](pile-o-magic/commander.md) | The same shared-library procedures, with 40 life, singleton construction, and three commander-selection methods. |

Both pages include the underlying Pile procedures. The Commander page does not merely say “play Pile with commanders.”

## Building a box

Specify the Pile list, player count, Heap Land supply, and any resolutions to the recorded shared-library questions. A prebuilt Themed Pile can control the card pool; a Messy Pile instead collects contributions from the players.
