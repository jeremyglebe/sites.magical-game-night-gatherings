# Jumpstart

[All formats](../)

Jumpstart combines two prepared, themed twenty-card packets into one forty-card deck. Lands are already included, so players can choose their packets and start playing without a deck-building session.

Official Jumpstart packets and custom packets can both supply this play structure. Custom boxes should state their card pool, packet-selection method, supported combinations, and any changes to the play rules. A packet's theme does not itself change how its cards work.

For this catalogue's reusable custom packets, every card receives its packet's small identifying sticker on the **front of an inner sleeve**, protected inside an **identical opaque-backed outer sleeve**. Matching symbols let players sort the packets after play while the card backs remain indistinguishable.
