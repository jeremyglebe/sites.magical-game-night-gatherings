# Archenemy

[All formats](../)

One archenemy uses a scheme deck against a team of heroes. Choose the version below: the life totals, deck requirements, and team rules differ.

| Ruleset | Decks | Starting life | Scheme deck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Standard Archenemy](variants/standard.md) | 60+ cards per player | Archenemy 40; each hero 20 | 20+ schemes; at most two of each name |
| [Archenemy Commander](variants/commander.md) | Commander decks | Archenemy 60; heroes share 60 | 10+ schemes; all different names |
| [Supervillain Rumble](variants/rumble.md) | 60+ cards per player | Everyone 40; free-for-all | Every player has 20+ schemes |
| [Commander Supervillain Rumble](variants/rumble-commander.md) | Commander decks | Everyone 40; free-for-all | Every player has 10+ unique schemes |

Each linked page includes its setup, scheme procedure, combat structure, and end-of-game rules.

## Building a box

A team-based Archenemy box can include a villain deck, its scheme deck, hero decks, tokens, and a scheme-timing reminder. State which ruleset it uses and record any changes to player count, life, or scheme composition on the box page. Campaigns, achievements, and difficulty adjustments are additional design work, not automatic Archenemy rules.
