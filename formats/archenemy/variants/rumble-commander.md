# Commander Supervillain Rumble

[Archenemy versions](../) · **Rules: Commander free-for-all**

## Players and materials

Play a free-for-all Commander game, normally with three or more players. Everyone has their own scheme deck.

Each player supplies a legal 100-card Commander deck, including its commander or commanders, with normal singleton and color-identity requirements. Each scheme deck contains **at least ten cards, all with different English names**.

## Setup

1. Put commanders in the command zone and shuffle ordinary libraries.
2. Shuffle scheme decks separately and keep them face down in their owners' command zones.
3. Set every player's individual life total to **40**.
4. Randomly choose the starting player.
5. Draw seven cards each and take normal multiplayer mulligans.

The 60-life team setup from Archenemy Commander does not apply to this free-for-all version.

## Turns, combat, and Commander rules

Take individual turns in normal turn order. Everyone is an opponent; nobody shares turns, life, or resources. The starting player draws on their first turn.

At the beginning of each player's precombat main phase, that player sets a scheme in motion. In the procedure below, “the archenemy” means that active player.

Attack any legal opponent or attackable object, assigning each attacker separately. Players may attack several opponents in one combat, but cannot block for another opponent. Commander tax, command-zone movement, and the 21-combat-damage loss condition from a single commander apply normally.

## Winning, losing, and leaving

Win by being the last remaining player or through a normal winning effect. Individual loss conditions include zero life, ten poison counters, drawing from an empty library, and 21 combat damage from a single commander.

When a player leaves, their owned objects, including schemes, leave under the ordinary multiplayer rules. Remaining players continue; if all remaining players lose together, the game is a draw.
