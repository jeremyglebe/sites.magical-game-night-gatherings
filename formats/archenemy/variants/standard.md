# Standard Archenemy

[Archenemy versions](../) · **Rules: traditional team game**

## Players and materials

Use one archenemy and a team of heroes. The published format recommends three or more heroes; the comprehensive rules allow a team of any size.

Each player supplies an ordinary constructed deck of at least 60 cards, using the group's agreed card pool and the normal four-copy limit except basic lands and card-specific exceptions. The archenemy also needs at least 20 scheme cards, with at most two cards of any one English name. Supply tokens and counters required by the cards.

## Setup

1. Choose the archenemy and the hero team.
2. Shuffle every ordinary deck and the scheme deck separately.
3. Set the archenemy to **40 life** and each hero to **20 life**. Hero life totals are separate.
4. Draw seven cards each and take normal multiplayer mulligans.
5. Put the face-down scheme deck in the command zone. No scheme starts in motion unless another effect says so.
6. The archenemy takes the first turn.

## Winning, losing, and leaving

The archenemy wins when every hero has lost. The hero team wins when the archenemy loses, including heroes who were eliminated earlier. If the archenemy and the last hero lose simultaneously, the game is a draw.

Each player's ordinary loss conditions still apply: zero or less life, drawing from an empty library, ten poison counters, or a relevant card effect. A hero losing does not eliminate the other heroes.

When a player leaves, remove their owned objects, end effects that gave them control of other players' objects, and apply the remaining multiplayer leaving-the-game rules. Do not simply leave an eliminated hero's creatures under the team's control.

## Other versions and references

For Commander decks with shared hero life, use [Archenemy Commander](commander.md). For everyone having schemes, use [Supervillain Rumble](rumble.md).
