# Archenemy Commander

[Archenemy versions](../) · **Rules: Commander team game**

## Players and materials

Choose one archenemy and a hero team, normally three or more heroes. Every player uses a legal Commander deck: 100 cards including the commander or commanders, the applicable color identity, and singleton construction except basic lands and explicit card exceptions.

The archenemy needs a scheme deck of **at least ten cards with different English names**. Prepare the tokens, counters, and commander-damage records required by the decks.

## Setup

1. Choose the archenemy and hero team.
2. Put each player's commander or commanders in their command zone. Shuffle ordinary libraries.
3. Shuffle the archenemy's scheme deck and keep it face down in the command zone.
4. The archenemy starts at **60 life**. The hero team starts with **one shared total of 60 life**, regardless of the number of heroes.
5. Each player draws seven and takes normal multiplayer mulligans.
6. The archenemy takes the first turn.

Commander tax, commander-zone movement, and commander damage remain in effect.

## Shared hero life and individual resources

Damage, life gain, and life loss affect the relevant player, then change the shared hero life total. A spell affecting each player or each opponent affects each hero separately; do not count the entire hero team as one target.

For example, if each of three heroes loses 2 life, their shared total falls by 6.

A cost or effect asking for a hero's life total uses the shared total. Paying life spends that shared resource; simultaneous life payments cannot exceed it. Setting one hero's life total adjusts the shared total accordingly. When an effect would set every hero's life, the team chooses one member to be affected. Teammates cannot exchange life totals with one another; redistribution can affect at most one member per team.

An effect preventing a hero from gaining life prevents the team's members from gaining life. An effect preventing a hero from losing life prevents team life loss and nonzero life payments. Apply the detailed shared-life rules in CR 810.9 for these interactions.

Poison counters are **individual**, with a threshold of **ten**, rather than the shared fifteen-poison rule from Two-Headed Giant. Record commander damage separately for each commander and each damaged player; do not pool damage from different commanders or different heroes.

## Winning and losing

Defeat the opposing side. This version uses the shared-life team win/loss rules referenced by CR 904.13b and 810.8: a team wins or loses together, including when one of its members wins or loses through an effect or another ordinary loss condition. A shared life total of zero or less causes the hero team to lose unless an applicable effect prevents it.

CR 904.13c specifically replaces shared poison with individual counters and the ten-counter threshold. Ordinary Commander damage remains a loss condition. Effects preventing a team member from winning or losing affect that team's result under the team rules. Conceding follows those team rules as well.

If both sides lose simultaneously, the game is a draw.

## Other versions and references
