# Booster-Draft Cube

[Cube parent page](../) · **Rules: ordinary booster drafting with a reusable card pool**

## Players and materials

Prepare a curated cube, an adequate supply of basic lands, and tokens or counters for its cards. Eight players is a common draft size; the pool needs at least **45 cards per player** for the three-pack, fifteen-card setup below.

A cube's theme, rarity restrictions, power level, and choice to use duplicates are construction decisions. They do not change the draft procedure unless the owner explicitly adds a rule.

## Prepare the draft

1. Choose the cube list and any announced special rules.
2. Randomize its cards and make **three fifteen-card packs for each player**.
3. Keep unused cube cards outside the draft.
4. Seat players in a circle and give each player their three packs.
5. Everyone opens only the first pack.

The owner may use deliberately seeded packs, but must specify that construction method. It is not part of the randomized default.

## Draft pack one

1. Look privately at the cards in the current pack.
2. Choose **one card** and place it face down in your drafted pool.
3. Pass the remaining cards to the player on your **left**.
4. Receive the next pack from your right and repeat.
5. Continue until every card from the first round of packs has been selected.

Keep selections separate from packs being passed so cards cannot be accidentally returned to a pack.

## Draft packs two and three

Open pack two when everyone has finished the first round. Repeat the same one-card-per-pick procedure, but pass to the **right**.

For pack three, pass to the **left** again.

Each player finishes with **45 drafted cards**. A procedure that takes two cards at a time, drafts commanders separately, or passes in a different pattern is a separate variant and must be announced.

## Build decks

- Build a deck of **at least 40 cards** from your drafted pool.
- Add ordinary basic lands from the land supply as needed.
- You may play any number of copies of a card you actually drafted; the constructed four-copy limit does not apply.
- Your undrafted remainder is not available. Your drafted cards that are not in the main deck form your sideboard.
- You may change the deck using that sideboard and the supplied basic lands between games, keeping at least 40 cards.

Drafting a legendary creature does not automatically turn this into Commander. This ruleset does not create a command zone, color-identity restriction, commander tax, or commander-damage condition.

## Play games

Choose pairings and a match structure before play. In normal two-player games, each player starts at **20 life**, draws **seven cards**, and uses normal mulligans. Determine who chooses to play or draw normally; the player taking the first turn skips their first draw step.

Play ordinary Magic turns and combat with normal victory and loss conditions. If the group wants multiplayer free-for-all matches instead, announce that structure and use its normal multiplayer setup.

## Reset the cube

At the end of play, return every drafted card to the cube, separate the borrowed basic lands, and check that tokens and other components have been collected. The cube is reusable; drafting does not transfer ownership of its cards.

## Other cube structures

Powered, unpowered, peasant, pauper, set, and theme cubes can all use this procedure. Commander Cube, Winston draft, grid draft, sealed pools, and rotisserie draft require their own additional procedures; mentioning those possibilities is not a complete ruleset for them.
