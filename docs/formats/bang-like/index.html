BANG-Like Hidden Role Formats ​

All formats

These games add secret roles and competing objectives to multiplayer Magic. Choose a complete version below. The leader's bonus, role visibility, rewards, and victory rules vary by version.

RulesetPlayersRoles and distinguishing rules
BANG! Magic5–7+, with agreed role countsSheriff, Deputy, Outlaw, Renegade; simple hidden roles.
Kingdoms5–6Basic-land role cards; the Usurper can replace the King.
Kingdom / BANG EDH5–6Emperor bonuses, Assassin rewards, Guard penalty, and a source ambiguity in the Rogue's victory wording.
MTG Treachery4+, with published setups through 8Custom Identity cards, unveil abilities, and formal team rules.

Related boxed sets ​

Feudalism is a draft monarch-focused box. Its page records the recurring crown rule and the base-version and role decisions still needed.

A hidden-role box normally needs role cards, a player-count chart, the chosen ruleset, and any extra reminder cards. Unless its rules explicitly tie a deck to a role, each included deck should function with any role.
