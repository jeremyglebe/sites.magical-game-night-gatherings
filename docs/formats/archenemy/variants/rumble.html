Supervillain Rumble ​

Archenemy versions · Rules: constructed free-for-all

Players and materials ​

This is the free-for-all version of Archenemy, normally for three or more players. Every player is an archenemy and supplies:

  • An ordinary constructed deck of at least 60 cards, following the agreed card pool and normal copy limits.
  • A separate scheme deck of at least 20 cards, with no more than two cards of any one English name.
  • Required tokens, counters, and life-tracking aids.

Setup ​

  1. Shuffle each ordinary deck and scheme deck separately.
  2. Keep each scheme deck face down in its owner's command zone.
  3. Every player starts at 40 life.
  4. Randomly determine the starting player.
  5. Draw seven cards each and take normal multiplayer mulligans.

Turns and combat ​

Take individual turns in ordinary turn order. There is no hero team, shared team turn, or shared life total. The starting player draws on their first turn.

Each player sets a scheme in motion at the beginning of their own precombat main phase. In the procedure below, “the archenemy” means the player whose turn it is.

Players may attack multiple opponents in the same combat, choosing the defending player or other legal attack destination for each creature. You cannot block for another opponent. Apply ordinary free-for-all rules.

Setting schemes in motion ​

Keep the scheme deck shuffled, face down in the command zone, separate from the ordinary library. Its owner cannot inspect or rearrange it without an effect allowing that.

As the archenemy's precombat main phase begins on each of their turns, turn the top scheme face up in the command zone. This mandatory turn-based action sets the scheme in motion and does not use the stack. Abilities that trigger from it do use the stack, so players can respond to those abilities.

Choose targets when putting a triggered ability on the stack. Make choices or payments that the effect asks for during resolution at that time, rather than while revealing the scheme.

Schemes stay in the command zone. They are not battlefield permanents, cannot be cast, and cannot be destroyed with an effect that destroys a permanent. A face-up scheme's abilities function from the command zone.

Non-ongoing schemes ​

A face-up, non-ongoing scheme goes face down on the bottom of its owner's scheme deck as a state-based action once no scheme triggered abilities are on the stack or waiting to be put there. Countering its ability does not leave the scheme permanently face up.

Ongoing schemes ​

An ongoing scheme stays face up until an ability tells its controller to abandon it. Abandoning it turns it face down and puts it on the bottom of its owner's scheme deck. Its printed condition determines when that happens; revealing another scheme does not automatically abandon it.

Follow individual scheme instructions for additional schemes, costs, choices, and exceptions. A scheme asking an opponent to choose “self” or “others” still permits “others” when that opponent has no teammates, even if that option affects nobody.

Winning, losing, and leaving ​

Win by being the last remaining player or through a normal winning effect. Ordinary Magic loss conditions apply, including zero life, ten poison counters, and drawing from an empty library.

When a player leaves, their owned cards, including their schemes, leave the game; apply the rest of the multiplayer leaving rules. The surviving players continue. Simultaneous loss of all remaining players results in a draw.

References ​

Wizards' Supervillain Rumble rules and Comprehensive Rules 904.12.

For Commander decks and singleton scheme decks, use Commander Supervillain Rumble.
