Custom Jumpstart Storage System

Research index · Useful Products & Tools · Card storage calculator · Jumpstart · Packet preparation

Design notes updated 2026-10-21. Physical fit remains untested. This project plans a reusable twenty-four-packet Jumpstart environment for up to twelve players. Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes are the preferred product family, with 30S the preferred size and 37S an acceptable oversized candidate. APACALI remains an earlier alternative with unresolved interior dimensions.

Reviewed capacity comparison

The Google handover's thickness estimates are superseded for purchase decisions. Its arithmetic is preserved below, but its compressed 0.50–0.53 mm range has no measurement behind it for these sleeves. The card storage calculator supports published benchmarks, measured stacks, custom ranges, and an optional sleeve-layer estimate. Actual compressed and post-play samples remain the best way to answer the four requested packing conditions.

Double Sleeved's original testing reports practical 100-card depths of 68 mm for Gamegenic Inner + Matte Prime and 72 mm for Dragon Shield Perfect Fit + Matte. These yield 0.68 and 0.72 mm per card. They are published sleeve-combination benchmarks, not established compressed/uncompressed endpoints or measurements of this project's stickered cards. The guide measures sleeve film with a micrometer and distinguishes calculated film thickness from practical stacks, but does not specify a reproducible compression protocol.

Box Usable depth used Capacity at 0.72 mm/card Capacity at 0.68 mm/card
APACALI, estimated interior 18.428 mm 25 cards 27 cards
Burger Tokens 30S 18.5 mm 25 cards 27 cards
Burger Tokens 37S 22.5 mm 31 cards 33 cards

These are total uniform-card capacities, rounded down, with no handling margin. Twenty cards occupy 13.6–14.4 mm across these two benchmarks; twenty-five occupy 17–18 mm. At twenty-five cards, APACALI has approximately 0.43–1.43 mm remaining, 30S 0.5–1.5 mm, and 37S 4.5–5.5 mm. Different inserts need their own thickness inputs. If APACALI's alternative 0.80-inch exterior depth is literal, its estimated 17.920 mm interior instead holds 24–26 cards at these benchmarks.

Depth capacity is conditional on face fit. Gamegenic publishes 66 × 91 mm for its outer sleeves, smaller than the handover's generic 68 × 94 mm assumption. That example fits both Burger dimensions on paper. The APACALI wall model leaves only 0.18 mm total width clearance for a 66 mm sleeve, so its face fit is especially uncertain. Neither this sleeve example nor these depth benchmarks verifies every sleeve brand or the actual molded box.

Project requirements

  • Prepare 24 theme packets, supplying two packets each to 12 simultaneous players. This means 480 deck cards; it does not prescribe a twelve-player game.
  • Each packet contains exactly 20 deck cards, including lands. The storage target is 25 total cards or inserts maximum, counting title cards, references, tokens, and art. The extra items stay separate from the deck during play.
  • Double-sleeve every deck card and put its packet's tracking sticker on the inner sleeve. Follow the existing protected sticker and identical outer-sleeve method.
  • Use a flattening tool to compress the sleeved packets and reduce trapped air, as described in the handover. The Sturdy Deck Press is a suggested 3D-printable option; its screw travel may require adding bulk cards beneath a short packet. Capacity must also accommodate packets after play; complete air removal and a fixed final thickness are unverified.
  • Prefer a container that is slightly oversized to one that is too tight.
  • Aim for a self-contained booster-style unboxing experience, with useful and thematic inserts providing some extra depth. The owner's later clarification also accepts padding or some loose space inside an intentionally larger box. A snug fit is optional; a final padding material has not been chosen.

Preferred product and larger fallback

The owner prefers Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes, based on their strong reputation in the Jumpstart subreddit. The owner reports that 30S is often favored for double-sleeved packets or extra tokens, and that stock shortages have been the main obstacle. This is the owner's community research and preference, rather than a claim that every Jumpstart player uses the same product.

The manufacturer labels these sizes 30 sleeved and 37 sleeved. It does not explicitly specify single versus double sleeves in those capacity labels. Use the stated dimensions and the complete packet's measured thickness rather than treating the label as a guaranteed double-sleeved count.

Size Internal dimensions External dimensions Store availability checked 2026-10-21
30S 67 × 95.5 × 18.5 mm 67.8 × 96.3 × 19.3 mm Unavailable
37S 67 × 95.5 × 22.5 mm 67.8 × 96.3 × 23.3 mm Available to order

External dimensions apply the manufacturer's instruction to add 0.8 mm to each internal dimension. Availability comes from the store's live product data; it is a dated snapshot, not a stock guarantee. The page also contains generic backorder wording, so orderability alone does not establish a dispatch date. The earlier handover's stock concern about 37S is superseded by this check.

The 37S adds 4 mm of depth, approximately 22%, with the same footprint. With twenty-five equally sleeved items at the reviewed benchmarks above, 30S leaves 0.5–1.5 mm and 37S 4.5–5.5 mm. The 37S therefore offers a useful allowance for this project's preference for excess space over a tight fit. Actual packet measurements remain necessary; the handover's much larger compressed-stack gaps are preserved only as historical calculations below.

The earlier generic 68 mm sleeved-width estimate exceeds the manufacturer's 67 mm interior width. That estimate was never an actual sleeve measurement. Replace it with the chosen outer sleeves' width before concluding whether either size fits; both sizes have identical face clearance. Burger Tokens reports testing its sleeved boxes with Dragon Shield, Ultra Pro Eclipse, and Fantasy Flight Games sleeves.

Both sizes are sold in ten-packs. Three ten-packs supply the 24 required boxes plus six spares. On the check date, the listed $12 per ten-pack and advertised $2 discount per pack when buying at least three imply $30 for 30 boxes before tax and any shipping, subject to checkout terms.

For the 37S, the owner is open to a removable spacer or other padding, extra presentation material within the existing insert budget, or simply accepting movement. No padding material or final purchase has been selected. Padding does not require increasing the twenty-card deck or the twenty-five-card/insert target.

Earlier APACALI candidate

The owner identified this APACALI Amazon listing as the product under closest consideration on 2026-10-21. Its product ID is B09CH5VK1G. The page retrieved that day selected 24 boxes at 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.82 inches, with advertised 1.2 mm plastic walls and a transparent hinged snap closure. Its description names polypropylene. These are seller specifications; no sample has been measured.

The same page lists a separate 30-box, 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.94-inch option, product ID B0B2JWTXLX. The 0-94 text in the supplied URL does not identify the selected option. Retain the product ID and selected option together when revisiting the listing.

Interior and exterior dimensions conflict

The handover labels 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.82 inches as internal dimensions. A Ubuy product listing also describes that size as internal, alongside 96 × 68 × 21 mm. However, the Amazon page's product-description text labels 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.8 inches as external dimensions. The units and descriptions are inconsistent; usable interior dimensions remain unresolved.

The exact conversion of 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.82 inches is 96.52 × 68.58 × 20.828 mm. This conversion does not establish which surfaces were measured or the manufacturing tolerances.

  • If those are internal dimensions: the handover's assumed 94 × 68 mm sleeved footprint leaves only 2.52 mm lengthwise and 0.58 mm widthwise in total. Centered, the width clearance is 0.29 mm per side.
  • If those are external dimensions: a simple rectangular-wall estimate subtracting two 1.2 mm walls gives about 66.18 mm internal width, too narrow for that assumed 68 mm footprint. This is a conditional calculation, not a measurement of the actual molded box.

Depth alone cannot establish fit. Check the narrowest interior, corners, lid, and latch against the actual sleeves. The handover's promises of zero friction, corner bending, or seam splitting are unsupported by its measurements.

For the capacity comparison below, the owner chose to treat the advertised size as external and estimate the interior. Using the selected option's 0.82-inch depth and subtracting two 1.2 mm walls gives 18.428 mm usable depth, with an estimated interior face of 94.12 × 66.18 mm. If the description's 0.80-inch depth is literal rather than rounded, the same method gives 17.920 mm usable depth. Neither estimate is a measurement of the lid or molded interior.

Card stack estimates from the handover

Historical assumptions, superseded for purchase decisions: this section preserves Google's original estimates and the corrections to its arithmetic. Use the reviewed comparison above or actual measurements in the calculator for current planning.

The earlier discussion assumes a roughly 68 × 94 mm double-sleeved footprint. Measure the chosen outer sleeves to replace that estimate. Its nominal bare-card reference is 63 × 88 mm; the exact inch conversion is about 2.48 × 3.46 inches, while 2.5 × 3.5 inches is a rounded description.

The thickness assumptions are 0.50–0.53 mm per compressed card and 0.55–0.70 mm per uncompressed or freshly played card. They are planning estimates from Google's AI, not measured results for these sleeves and stickers.

Contents and condition Estimated thickness Rounded inches
20 cards, compressed 10.00–10.60 mm 0.39–0.42 in
20 cards, uncompressed 11.00–14.00 mm 0.43–0.55 in
25 cards, compressed 12.50–13.25 mm 0.49–0.52 in
25 cards, uncompressed 13.75–17.50 mm 0.54–0.69 in

The arithmetic is consistent with those assumptions. Actual thickness also depends on the inserts' stock, their sleeves, and the identifying stickers. Stickers aligned in the same corner can create a locally thicker stack. Measure the complete packet at its thickest point, both after compression and after play.

The handover's 20.828 mm internal-depth assumption would leave 3.328 mm around a 17.50 mm stack. Under the owner's requested external-dimension model, the APACALI allowance is instead 0.928 mm, as calculated below.

Estimated card capacity and the four packing conditions

Historical calculation using the unverified Google thickness ranges. These figures explain the handover; they are not the reviewed capacity recommendation above. No measured compressed/uncompressed pair has yet been supplied for this project's sleeves and stickers.

The requested four conditions are twenty or twenty-five cards, each compressed or uncompressed. Starting with twenty instead of twenty-five cards changes the remaining space, but not a box's total capacity. Calculate total capacity as floor(usable depth / thickness per card). The lower end uses thicker cards and the upper end thinner cards from the handover's ranges.

Card condition APACALI estimated 18.428 mm interior Burger Tokens 30S 18.5 mm interior Burger Tokens 37S 22.5 mm interior
Compressed, 0.50–0.53 mm per card 34–36 cards 34–37 cards 42–45 cards
Uncompressed, 0.55–0.70 mm per card 26–33 cards 26–33 cards 32–40 cards

These are theoretical depth capacities with no extra removal allowance. They use the earlier AI's thickness estimates, not a test of this project's sleeves or a manufacturer capacity rating. The fact that this model predicts more than thirty double-sleeved cards in a box labeled 30 sleeved is a reason to measure the real stack before relying on the upper values. A box's width and height must also accommodate the actual sleeve.

20 uncompressed cards 11.00–14.00 mm 4.43–7.43 mm 4.50–7.50 mm 8.50–11.50 mm
25 compressed cards 12.50–13.25 mm 5.18–5.93 mm 5.25–6.00 mm 9.25–10.00 mm

If the APACALI exterior is actually 0.80 inch deep, its modeled capacities become 33–35 compressed cards or 25–32 uncompressed cards, one fewer at each end. Subtract 0.508 mm from its remaining-depth figures above.

For this calculation, APACALI and 30S provide nearly identical depth. The 37S provides 4 mm more than 30S, equivalent to approximately 7–8 additional compressed cards or 5–7 additional uncompressed cards, depending on thickness. A twenty-five-card compressed packet would leave roughly 9–10 mm in the 37S, making the owner's padding or loose-fit options relevant. Title cards and tokens only follow these estimates if their stock and sleeving produce the assumed thickness.

Presentation and insert budget

Preserve this front-to-back arrangement:

  1. A theme title card, facing outward through the clear front, such as GOBLINS.
  2. A player reference card explaining the turn sequence.
  3. The twenty-card Jumpstart packet.
  4. Thematic tokens and full-art cards, with the last visible artwork facing the clear back.

The handover proposes 20 deck cards + 1 title + 1 guide + 4–5 token or art cards. That totals 26–27 items, exceeding its own 25-item maximum. Keeping the maximum and both front inserts leaves at most three token or art cards. Their final allocation is unresolved; the cap has not been increased.

The original depth example was 10.3 mm for the deck, 1.1 mm for the title and guide, and 2.6 mm for the token/art group: 14.0 mm total. Against the assumed 20.828 mm interior, that fills 67.2% and leaves 6.828 mm. Twenty compressed cards at 10.3 mm fill approximately half, not exactly half.

Extra cards reduce the unused depth but do not immobilize the contents. A 6.8 mm gap permits movement and does not itself guarantee finger access. Retain the presentation preference and test removal and movement with a complete sample. Under the handover's compressed-card assumptions, a compliant 25-item stack would instead be 12.50–13.25 mm, provided every insert has the same thickness as a sleeved deck card.

Measurements needed for the next design step

Record the exact sleeve models, the maximum sleeved width and height, the sticker type, and the complete packet's compressed and post-play thickness. For a sample box, record the minimum usable interior dimensions and maximum closed exterior dimensions, including hinges and latches. Confirm the packet enters, the lid closes without pressure on the contents, and cards can be removed comfortably.

Master carrying-case dimensions require the closed exterior box measurements, plus space for dividers, padding, and access. Internal packet-box dimensions cannot size the carrying case. For a row of n boxes, the planning formula is:

row length = n × exterior box dimension along the row
           + (n − 1) × divider or gap allowance
           + two end margins

The original discussion suggested theme-title templates and a master-case layout as possible next tasks. Neither has been chosen or designed here. Across 24 packets, the current cap permits 600 total cards or inserts: 480 deck cards and up to 120 extras.
