Everyday Marvel and Spider-Man Cards

Ordinary life drawn from the Marvel card pool, checked October 19, 2026. These are full individual card entries, including artifacts, Vehicles, lands, instants, sorceries, and enchantments as well as creatures.

Direct means a strong ordinary-life premise; Setting means the subject is ordinary but less suited to present-day New York. Marvel's powered pets, civilian superhero identities, and other qualified exceptions appear in Almost matches. See the catalogue guide for scope and rules distinctions.

People and jobs

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Alicia Masters, Skilled Sculptor · MSC 48 Legendary Creature — Human Artificer Direct A working sculptor; an ordinary civilian gets a named role alongside the superheroes.
Angry Rabble · SPM 75 Creature — Human Citizen Direct An angry crowd of city residents. Human Citizen is the actual type line.
Aunt May · MSC 768 Legendary Creature — Human Citizen Direct A family member looking after the neighborhood; the Spider bonus does not make her a Spider creature.
Bold Biochemist · MSH 48 Creature — Human Scientist Direct A scientist at work. Research is an ordinary profession even in a superhero setting.
Cheering Crowd · SPM 126 Creature — Human Citizen Direct Spectators become the main event; the ability involves decisions by multiple players.
Common Crook · MSC 792 Creature — Human Rogue Villain Direct An everyday thief, explicitly a Human Rogue Villain; Villain does not necessarily mean superpowered.
Crowd of True Believers · MSH 14 Creature — Human Citizen Direct Fans and spectators providing moral support.
Daily Bugle Reporters · MSC 771 Creature — Human Citizen Direct Working journalists, with reporting-themed modes.
Damage Control Crew · SPM 99 Creature — Human Citizen Direct The people who have to repair and clear up the city after everyone else's adventure.
Doc Ock's Henchmen · MSC 784 Creature — Human Villain Direct Hired muscle with a paycheck and a reluctance to fight Spider-Man.
Everett K. Ross, Hapless Attaché · MSC 13 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Direct A government liaison. His commander-specific ability still needs the chosen format to make sense.
Flash Thompson, Spider-Fan · MSC 772 Legendary Creature — Human Citizen Direct This particular version is a civilian fan, distinct from Flash's powered identities.
Foggy Nelson, On Retainer · MSC 594 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Direct A lawyer: ideal for the legal-bills side of a city box.
Gallant Citizen · MSC 820 Creature — Human Citizen Direct A helpful ordinary citizen, not a Hero by creature type.
Guy in the Chair · SPM 102 Creature — Human Advisor Direct The friend providing technical support; the Spider-specific ability is a deck-building consideration.
Happy Hogan, Bodyguard · MSC 622 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Direct A bodyguard doing his job, with no superhuman identity on this card.
Happy Hogan, Dauntless Driver · MSC 545 Legendary Creature — Human Pilot Direct A named driver; particularly appropriate for a transit or service-worker packet.
J. Jonah Jameson · SPM 81 Legendary Creature — Human Citizen Direct The newspaper boss. Suspecting a pigeon is a possible game interaction, not a new house rule.
Jarvis, Earth's Mightiest Butler · MSC 34 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Direct A butler whose employers happen to be superheroes; his draw ability specifically needs Hero spells.
Kingpin's Enforcers · MSH 102 Creature — Human Villain Direct Organized crime's collection department: ordinary people with an unpleasant job.
Mary Jane Watson · SPM 134 Legendary Creature — Human Performer Direct A civilian performer; the card rewards Spiders but is itself Human Performer.
Merciless Enforcers · SPM 58 Creature — Human Mercenary Villain Direct Street-level hired muscle, useful for ordinary-criminal themes.
MJ, Rising Star · MSC 773 Legendary Creature — Human Performer Direct The acting career rather than a superhero identity; a separate card from Mary Jane Watson.
Mob Lookout · MSC 821 Creature — Human Rogue Villain Direct Someone watching the street while a crime takes place.
Night Nurse, Healer of Heroes · MSH 26 Legendary Creature — Human Doctor Hero Direct An ordinary medical professional treating extraordinary patients. Hero appears in the type line; that alone does not mean superpowers.
Oscorp Research Team · SPM 40 Creature — Human Scientist Direct Ordinary employees doing research for a spectacularly questionable company.
Professional Wrestler · SPM 110 Creature — Human Warrior Performer Direct A sports entertainer, with Performer and Warrior types rather than Citizen.
Queen Mother Ramonda · MSC 23 Legendary Creature — Human Noble Setting An unpowered human ruler, but royalty and Wakanda are a stretch from everyday New York. Introduces the monarch rules.
Quinjet Technician · MSC 703 Creature — Human Artificer Direct An aircraft mechanic. The specialist mana ability supports power-up cards.
Raft Security Officer · MSH 33 Creature — Human Soldier Direct A prison guard; the job is ordinary even though the inmates and equipment are not.
Stark Industries Executive · MSH 153 Creature — Human Advisor Direct Corporate administration and financing, rather than a suit of superhero armor.
Supportive Parents · SPM 119 Creature — Human Citizen Direct Parents helping their child: one of the clearest everyday-life cards.
Taxi Driver · MSC 811 Creature — Human Pilot Direct Exactly the urban transport worker the theme wants.
The Mary Janes · SPE 15 Legendary Creature — Human Bard Performer Direct A band. The members' other stories do not change this card's musical premise.
Unlucky Witness · MSC 812 Creature — Human Citizen Direct Someone who saw the wrong thing at the wrong time.
Willie Lumpkin, Postman · MSC 101 Legendary Creature — Human Citizen Direct Postal delivery continues even when the city is full of superheroes.

Animals

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Birds of Paradise · MSC 170 Creature — Bird Setting Ordinary birds in a Marvel printing; wildlife rather than a pigeon on the subway. Inspect the particular art rather than inferring the species from the card name.
City Pigeon · MSC 769 Creature — Bird Direct The central city-wildlife joke. Bird, not Citizen; its departure creates a Food token.
Kraven's Cats · SPM 104 Creature — Cat Villain Setting Trained big cats associated with a villain. More zoo or escaped-animal material than normal street pets.
Lucky the Pizza Dog · MSC 726 Legendary Creature — Dog Direct An ordinary named dog with an extremely suitable interest in food.
Wakandan Tusker · MSC 748 Creature — Elephant Setting An elephant rather than a superhero; better as zoo or wildlife material than a New York native.

Objects and transportation

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Bagel and Schmear · SPM 161 Artifact — Food Direct Breakfast as a game piece; an actual Food artifact with two ways to use it.
Daily Bugle Newspaper · MSC 749 Artifact Direct A physical newspaper, distinct from the building and the reporters.
Hot Dog Cart · SPM 164 Artifact Direct Street food and a mana source in one ordinary piece of city equipment.
News Helicopter · SPM 169 Artifact Creature — Construct Direct Local news transport. It is an artifact creature — Construct, not a Vehicle, and it creates a Citizen token.
Passenger Ferry · SPM 170 Artifact — Vehicle Direct Ordinary public transportation; an actual Vehicle with crew 2.
Peter Parker's Camera · SPM 171 Artifact Direct Ordinary photography equipment with film counters, despite its famous owner's name.
Pictures of Spider-Man · SPM 109 Artifact Direct Selling photographs to a newspaper is mundane even when the subject is a superhero.
Steel Wrecking Ball · SPM 177 Artifact Direct A real demolition tool; the surrounding superhero action does not change the object's ordinary nature.
Subway Train · SPM 178 Artifact — Vehicle Direct The commute becomes a win condition. Vehicle with crew 2, not a creature card in the library.
Wedding Ring · MAR 7 Artifact Direct An everyday ceremonial object. Its gameplay involves copying the ring and linking players, so check the format.

Places

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Daily Bugle Building · SPM 179 Land Direct A newspaper office in the city; pairs naturally with its staff and paper.
Fogwell's Gym · MSC 754 Land Direct An ordinary boxing gym with an especially good street-level identity.
Hell's Kitchen · MSH 268 Land Direct A real New York neighborhood, represented as a land.
Suburban Sanctuary · SPM 185 Land Direct A family home. More neighborhood/suburban than downtown, but clearly ordinary life.
University Campus · SPM 186 Land Direct Higher education and homework, with a city-appropriate setting.
Urban Retreat · SPM 187 Land Direct Apartment hunting and somewhere to live; an excellent companion to Rent Is Due.
Vibrant Cityscape · SPM 188 Land Direct The city itself, without requiring a named superhero headquarters.

Everyday activities

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Call Damage Control · MSH 162 Sorcery Direct Calling the repair crew after something has gone wrong.
Friendly Neighborhood · SPM 8 Enchantment — Aura Direct An Aura that supplies ordinary Citizen tokens and a community-support ability.
Parker Luck · SPM 60 Enchantment Direct Bad luck and life refusing to go to plan. The particular unlucky resident happens to be Peter Parker.
Political Triumph · MSH 31 Enchantment — Plan Direct Politics as a city-life theme. It is an Enchantment — Plan, not an instant or sorcery.
Rent Is Due · SPM 11 Enchantment Direct An enchantment about paying rent: one of the strongest everyday New York jokes.
Romantic Rendezvous · MSC 808 Sorcery Direct Going on a date; no need to remove the card's normal draw/discard effect.
School Daze · SPM 42 Instant Direct Exact spelling is Daze. An instant with Do Homework and Fight Crime modes; the homework premise qualifies, and both modes remain legal.
Scout the City · MSC 817 Sorcery Direct Looking around town; its alternate removal mode does not erase that ordinary premise.
Wisecrack · MSC 813 Instant Direct Making a joke at someone's expense. Superhero context, ordinary human behavior.
