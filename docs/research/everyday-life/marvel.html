Everyday Marvel and Spider-Man Cards ​

Ordinary life drawn from the Marvel card pool, checked October 19, 2026. These are full individual card entries, including artifacts, Vehicles, lands, instants, sorceries, and enchantments as well as creatures.

Direct means a strong ordinary-life premise; Setting means the subject is ordinary but less suited to present-day New York. Marvel's powered pets, civilian superhero identities, and other qualified exceptions appear in Almost matches. See the catalogue guide for scope and rules distinctions.
