Food, Objects, and Transportation Beyond Marvel ​

Meals, tools, household objects, games, and vehicles, checked October 19, 2026. These are ordinary things that can become ridiculous game pieces without needing to be characters. The Marvel page contains the bagel, hot dog cart, camera, newspaper, news helicopter, ferry, and subway.

Direct entries have a strong everyday-life premise; Setting entries have a more historical or fantastical visual context. Animated objects, disguised machines, and other explicit exceptions are in Almost matches. Vehicles retain their normal crew and animation rules.
