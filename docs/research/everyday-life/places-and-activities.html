Places and Everyday Activities Beyond Marvel

Streets, businesses, homes, work, meals, school, social events, and ordinary crime, checked October 19, 2026. This page includes instants, sorceries, enchantments, and lands. A theme about everyday life needs things for its people to do, not only creatures to attack with.

Direct entries have a strong ordinary-life premise. Setting entries require a visual or era stretch. Supernatural Rooms, magical cooking, and other explicit exceptions appear under Almost matches. Rent Is Due and School Daze are on the Marvel page.

Back to the full catalogue.

Places

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
Adventurer's Inn · FIN 271 Land — Town Setting An ordinary lodging business for fantasy adventurers.
Big Apple, 3 a.m. · TMC 42 Land Direct New York at night, with an ability that creates Rat tokens. One of the strongest cross-franchise lands.
Botanical Plaza · SNC 247 Land Setting A city garden/plaza, with a fantasy Art Deco visual setting.
Cabaretti Courtyard · EOC 151 Land Setting A social courtyard associated with a fantasy-city crime family.
Concealed Courtyard · OTJ 268 Land Setting A private urban courtyard, with a world-specific visual style.
Demolition Field · FDN 687 Land Setting A site being torn down; potential urban-redevelopment flavor depending on the printing.
Illegitimate Business · TMT 186 Land Direct An ordinary-looking city business with a criminal secret, from TMNT.
Jasmine Dragon Tea Shop · TLA 270 Land Setting An ordinary tea shop from Avatar. A strong service-business crossover, with a different city and era.
Library of Alexandria · ARN 76 Land Setting A real historical kind of institution, not modern New York. No need to chase a costly printing for the theme.
Lush Portico · MKM 263 Land — Forest Plains Setting An ordinary architectural feature within a fantasy setting.
Maestros Theater · EOC 167 Land Setting A performance venue with a crime-family affiliation.
Night Market · DFT 258 Land Setting Shopping after dark in a fantasy setting.
Northampton Farm · TMT 188 Land Setting A real-world-style rural retreat from TMNT; useful broader everyday life, not urban New York.
Obscura Storefront · DSC 291 Land Setting A storefront with an ordinary city-business facade, in a fantasy crime setting.
Port Town · CMM 1019 Land Setting A coastal settlement rather than a superhero landmark; printing selection matters.
Public Thoroughfare · MKM 265 Land Setting A city street; a very useful ordinary infrastructure name.
Raucous Theater · MKM 266 Land — Swamp Mountain Setting A busy entertainment venue in a fantasy city.
Rishadan Pawnshop · MMQ 311 Artifact Setting An ordinary business in an older fantasy port city.
Rishadan Port · A25 246 Land Setting A working harbor; ordinary infrastructure, period-fantasy presentation.
Shadowy Backstreet · MKM 268 Land — Plains Swamp Setting An alley or backstreet in a fantasy city.
Skybridge Towers · SNC 256 Land Setting High-rise architecture and pedestrian infrastructure in New Capenna.
Tin Street Market · GTC 108 Enchantment — Aura Setting A neighborhood market; an Aura rather than a land despite the place name.
Tramway Station · SNC 258 Land Setting A public-transit stop in New Capenna; among the closest non-Marvel infrastructure matches.
Undercity Sewers · MKM 270 Land — Island Swamp Setting Urban sewage infrastructure; the Ravnican setting supplies the visual caveat.

Everyday activities

Card and printing Actual type Fit Why consider it / qualification
An Offer You Can't Refuse · FDN 160 Instant Setting A familiar organized-crime negotiation; prefer a city-crime printing over alternate art that changes the scene.
Barroom Brawl · CLB 217 Sorcery Setting An ordinary tavern fight, in a fantasy world rather than a modern bar.
Blackmail · 9ED 115 Sorcery Setting A recognizable crime and social threat; inspect the particular printing's scene.
Bouncer's Beatdown · SNC 135 Instant Setting A nightclub dispute handled by the bouncer; the New Capenna scene is the qualification.
Break Down the Door · DSK 170 Instant Setting A mundane act of forced entry in a supernatural-horror setting.
Bribery · CMM 77 Sorcery Setting Corruption and paying someone off; a familiar civic/crime theme with a potentially powerful effect.
Destroy the Evidence · RTR 64 Sorcery Setting An ordinary criminal cover-up, with fantasy-city imagery.
Extortion · MMQ 135 Sorcery Setting An ordinary criminal tactic, with older fantasy-era art.
Great Train Heist · OTJ 125 Instant Setting A robbery on public transportation, with a Western-era setting.
Grounded for Life · TMT 7 Instant Direct A parent's reprimand and household discipline, delivered in a TMNT scene.
Hard Evidence · MH2 46 Sorcery Setting Evidence gathering with a Crab token: particularly amusing alongside a waterfront or animal theme.
Highway Robbery · OTJ 129 Sorcery Setting An ordinary crime transplanted to a frontier setting.
Hold for Questioning · J22 11 Enchantment — Aura Setting Detaining someone to ask questions, without requiring a superhero premise.
Hold for Ransom · SNC 16 Enchantment — Aura Setting A mundane crime scenario in New Capenna.
Jailbreak · NCC 17 Sorcery Setting Escaping incarceration; New Capenna's setting is the main caveat.
Jailbreak Scheme · OTJ 53 Sorcery Setting Planning a prison escape in a frontier setting.
Join the Dance · INR 242 Sorcery Setting A social dance in a Gothic-period setting; an ordinary activity with two Human tokens.
Kick in the Door · AFR 153 Sorcery Setting An ordinary action with a fantasy-adventuring presentation.
Land Tax · SOC 153 Enchantment Setting Taxes are mundane, even if the printing's tax collector belongs to another era.
Mark of Eviction · RAV 58 Enchantment — Aura Setting A painfully ordinary housing problem, presented with Ravnican magical gameplay.
Ninja Pizza · TMC 32 Enchantment Direct A pizza-business/food theme despite Ninja in the name; mechanically an enchantment that supplies Food.
On the Job · MKM 30 Instant Setting People doing their jobs during a fantasy-city investigation.
One Last Job · OTJ 22 Sorcery Setting The familiar crime-story promise that this is the final job.
Outrageous Robbery · MKM 97 Instant Setting A city heist. The act is mundane even if the surrounding setting is fantastic.
Plan the Heist · OTJ 62 Sorcery Setting Planning a robbery, with a frontier rather than modern-city presentation.
Prizefight · SNC 154 Instant Setting A paid fight or boxing match in New Capenna.
Restock · M15 195 Sorcery Setting Replenishing supplies; inspect the older fantasy printing for what is being restocked.
Second Breakfast · LTR 29 Instant Setting An ordinary extra meal, associated with hobbits rather than modern city residents.
Spring Cleaning · LRW 236 Instant Setting An ordinary household chore, with a fairy-tale setting and clash mechanics.
Study Break · STX 34 Instant Setting Taking a break from schoolwork; the students attend a magical school, but the activity is ordinary.
Study the Classics · TMC 128 Sorcery Direct An ordinary leisure/study joke in a TMNT scene; the exact pictured classics can matter to the packet's tone.
Uncovered Clues · DGM 19 Sorcery Setting Investigation and evidence in a fantasy city.
Wash Away · VOW 87 Instant Setting Washing something away is mundane; the Gothic/magical scene is the qualification.
Well Rested · PIP 88 Enchantment — Aura Direct Getting a good rest, with Fallout branding and a post-apocalyptic setting to consider.
You've Been Caught Stealing · CLB 211 Sorcery Setting An ordinary crime consequence in a Dungeons & Dragons setting; retains its full normal modes.
