Pets, Pests, and Ordinary Wildlife ​

Cats, dogs, city birds, rats, squirrels, insects, and broader wildlife candidates, checked October 19, 2026. Modern city animals have priority, but bears, farm animals, exotic pets, and zoo possibilities are retained with their setting limitations.

Direct entries are especially close to the everyday-city animal idea. Setting entries are ordinary-animal candidates whose habitat, era, or presentation is a stretch. Supernatural pets, animated toys, giant pests, and joke-card exceptions have their own Almost matches section.

There are indeed cards literally named Cat, but the examples verified here are tokens. For ordinary deck cards, start with Sanctuary Cat, Helpful Hunter, Generous Stray, and Warehouse Tabby. See the separate token table for Cat and other animal token profiles.
