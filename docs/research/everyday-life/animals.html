Pets, Pests, and Ordinary Wildlife

Cats, dogs, city birds, rats, squirrels, insects, and broader wildlife candidates, checked October 19, 2026. Modern city animals have priority, but bears, farm animals, exotic pets, and zoo possibilities are retained with their setting limitations.

Direct entries are especially close to the everyday-city animal idea. Setting entries are ordinary-animal candidates whose habitat, era, or presentation is a stretch. Supernatural pets, animated toys, giant pests, and joke-card exceptions have their own Almost matches section.

There are indeed cards literally named Cat, but the examples verified here are tokens. For ordinary deck cards, start with Sanctuary Cat, Helpful Hunter, Generous Stray, and Warehouse Tabby. See the separate token table for Cat and other animal token profiles.

Thraben Purebloods · ISD 37 Creature — Dog Setting Dogs in a Gothic-fantasy town, not supernatural dogs by creature type.
Thriving Rats · KLD 102 Creature — Rat Setting Rats in Kaladesh's industrial world; plausible machine-room or factory pests.
Trufflesnout · M21 212 Creature — Boar Setting A pig rooting around for food; rural or petting-zoo material.
Trusty Retriever · JMP 8 Creature — Dog Direct A dog fetching something; very close to mundane pet behavior.
Tunnel Rats · TMT 84 Creature — Rat Direct Rats in the TMNT underground-city setting, rather than mutant humanoid turtles.
Typhoid Rats · FRF 89 Creature — Rat Setting A disease-carrying pest; ordinary danger rather than a superhero.
Vampire Bats · GVL 35 Creature — Bat Setting A real animal name; the creature type is Bat, not Vampire. Inspect the particular art before choosing it.
Vexing Gull · THB 79 Creature — Bird Setting A gull: an excellent annoying urban-waterfront animal, despite its original setting.
Voracious Vermin · WOE 116 Creature — Rat Setting A hungry rat and its infestation support, in a fantasy setting.
War Falcon · M13 38 Creature — Bird Setting A trained falcon with a soldiers/knights restriction that may be awkward in a civilian box.
Warehouse Tabby · WOE 117 Creature — Cat Direct A warehouse cat: an exceptionally close fit for ordinary city animals.
Welkin Tern · GS1 5 Creature — Bird Setting A seabird that can fit the waterfront, with fantasy-era artwork.
