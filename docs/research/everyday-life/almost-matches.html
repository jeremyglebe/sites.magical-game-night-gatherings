Almost Matches: Ordinary Things with an Exception ​

These cards are deliberately not filed as wholly mundane. Each preserves some part of the everyday-life joke, while the note identifies the powers, species, transformation, technology, setting, or special rules that make it a close call. Checked October 19, 2026.

Venomized Cat is the model example requested for this research: a little neighborhood cat acquired alien symbiote goo. That can be funny enough to include, and it remains a Symbiote Cat Villain. A thematic exception does not change a card's printed rules or a format's deck-construction requirements.

Silver-bordered cards are explicitly marked in their rows. They are optional research candidates, not automatic additions to a box using ordinary Magic cards. Unfinity sticker rules, where present, are also separate from the purely organizational Jumpstart inner-sleeve labels.
