Almost Matches: Ordinary Things with an Exception

These cards are deliberately not filed as wholly mundane. Each preserves some part of the everyday-life joke, while the note identifies the powers, species, transformation, technology, setting, or special rules that make it a close call. Checked October 19, 2026.

Venomized Cat is the model example requested for this research: a little neighborhood cat acquired alien symbiote goo. That can be funny enough to include, and it remains a Symbiote Cat Villain. A thematic exception does not change a card's printed rules or a format's deck-construction requirements.

Silver-bordered cards are explicitly marked in their rows. They are optional research candidates, not automatic additions to a box using ordinary Magic cards. Unfinity sticker rules, where present, are also separate from the purely organizational Jumpstart inner-sleeve labels.

Doctor Strange, Surgeon · MSC 591 Legendary Creature — Human Doctor Hero Almost The civilian medical career is the premise, but the character is also a sorcerer and this card has Hero in its type line.
Donald Blake, Guise of Thor · MSC 592 Legendary Creature — Human Doctor Almost Starts as a doctor but explicitly becomes a God Warrior Hero through its ability; not purely an ordinary person.
Fisk Tower · MSH 265 Land Almost Office-tower imagery with a very specific supervillain power structure behind it.
Glamorous Grapplers · MSC 536 Creature — Human Performer Villain Almost The professional-wrestling joke fits, but these are Marvel villain performers rather than a promise of ordinary athletes.
Heroes' Hangout · MSC 804 Sorcery Almost Date Night is thoroughly ordinary; Patrol Night and the heroes in the title make the mixed premise explicit.
Heroic Feast · MSH 172 Enchantment Almost Dinner is ordinary, but the gathering and presentation explicitly center heroes.
Hit-Monkey · MSC 723 Legendary Creature — Monkey Assassin Almost The small-animal joke survives, but an armed assassin monkey is decidedly extraordinary.
Inner Demons Gangsters · MSC 797 Creature — Human Rogue Villain Almost Street criminals with an explicitly supernatural affiliation. Useful if the gang aesthetic outweighs that connection.
Iron Suitcase · MSC 750 Artifact Almost Looks like luggage; its ability turns it into a flying Construct. A very clear ordinary-object/extraordinary-secret joke.
Masked Meower · MSC 807 Creature — Spider Cat Hero Almost A neighborhood cat doing superhero work, with Spider Cat Hero types. Funny pet premise, genuinely powered.
Matt Murdock, Justice Seeker · MSC 602 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Hero Almost The lawyer role fits everyday city life, but this is Daredevil's civilian identity, not an unpowered civilian.
Oscorp Industries · SPM 182 Land Almost An employer and office/laboratory complex, but tightly connected to superhero science and villains.
Pet Avengers · MSH 178 Creature — Dragon Cat Dog Bird Frog Hero Almost A team of pets with heroic and fantastical members; not a collection of ordinary animals.
Prison Break · SPM 61 Sorcery Almost A recognizable crime story, with a superhero-villain breakout in this printing.
Radioactive Spider · SPM 111 Creature — Spider Almost A small spider makes an excellent mundane-looking threat, but the radioactivity is the origin-story exception.
Rick Jones, Destined Sidekick · MSH 184 Legendary Creature — Human Advisor Almost A human sidekick on this card, with a much less ordinary wider Marvel history and explicit Hero support.
Risky Research · SPM 62 Sorcery Almost Laboratory work fits, but the radiation and villain-science framing are the qualification.
Roxxon Brutes · MSH 113 Creature — Human Berserker Villain Almost Corporate thugs are a strong city premise; their villain presentation and escalating strength make this a looser fit.
Secret Identity · SPM 43 Instant Almost Conceal becomes an ordinary Citizen; Reveal explicitly produces a flying Hero. Keep both modes unless a box deliberately changes a rule.
Spider-Mobile · MSC 826 Artifact — Vehicle Almost A car-shaped joke whose superhero equipment is part of the premise.
Stark Industries · MSH 272 Land Almost Corporate life fits; the company's extraordinary technology is a visible thematic qualification.
The Thanos-Copter · MSC 753 Legendary Artifact — Vehicle Almost A helicopter with an absurdly overqualified owner. Deliberately not anonymous civilian transport.
Tippy-Toe, Terrific Partner · MSC 734 Legendary Creature — Squirrel Hero Almost A little squirrel with a heroic team role; a good small-animal joke, explicitly a Squirrel Hero.
TVA Bureaucrat · MSC 761 Creature — Human Advisor Almost Bureaucracy is mundane; administering timelines is not. A particularly good workplace joke.
Venomized Cat · MSC 801 Creature — Symbiote Cat Villain Almost The owner's explicit near-match: an ordinary little cat acquired alien symbiote goo. Actual types include Symbiote and Villain.

Scrapwork Mutt · BRO 164 Artifact Creature — Dog Almost An assembled machine with a dog's shape, fitting a junkyard-pet joke.
Scurry Oak · MH2 172 Creature — Treefolk Almost A Treefolk that produces squirrels: useful support, but the creature itself is a walking tree.
Skyswimmer Koi · NEO 79 Creature — Fish Almost A pond fish that literally flies through the sky. Close visually, not a normal koi.
Spirited Companion · CMM 838 Enchantment Creature — Dog Almost A charming small dog, but specifically an enchantment creature from a spirit-filled setting.
Spore Frog · MH1 180 Creature — Frog Almost A frog with a fantastical defensive spore mechanism; small-animal comedy with a clear caveat.
Undercity Dire Rat · FIN 123 Creature — Rat Almost A rat in an urban underworld, but exaggerated Final Fantasy monster scale is the qualification.
Witch's Familiar · M15 123 Creature — Frog Almost The animal is a little frog, but its named role is explicitly magical.
