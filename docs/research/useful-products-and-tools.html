Useful Products & Tools

Saved products and tools for assembling, storing, and reusing game-night boxes. Keep the exact size and product identifier when reordering: stock, prices, and marketplace listings can change. Last reviewed: October 21, 2026.

For cube drafting, Dragon Shield Cube Shells and decorative Cubeamajigs provide reusable booster containers. For the twenty-four-packet Jumpstart project, Burger Tokens is the preferred box family. The storage research records the fit assumptions and comparisons; use the card storage calculator with the actual sleeves and inserts before choosing a size.

Storage boxes

Dragon Shield Cube Shells

Reusable booster containers for cube drafts. Dragon Shield specifies eight shells per retail pack, each holding fifteen double-sleeved cards or twenty single-sleeved cards. They have a secure closure and a writing field on top for labeling. These are the manufacturer's capacity claims, not measurements of this project's sleeves. Manufacturer's product page

  • Amazon listing — Blood Red, eight-pack
  • Reorder identifiers: ASIN B09W6CQPHH; model AT-30550; Dragon Shield Cube Shell — Blood Red.
  • Draft quantity: an eight-player booster-draft cube, using three fifteen-card packs per player, needs twenty-four shells: three retail eight-packs.

Find it again: use the manufacturer's product page or Dragon Shield's Shells collection, or search Dragon Shield Cube Shell Blood Red AT-30550. Match the Cube Shell name, color, and eight-pack quantity when reordering.

Cubeamajigs decorative booster packs

Reusable cube packs with printed artwork. The linked Land Panorama Set features art by Daarken, with five designs, two of each, for ten containers. Hit Point Press advertises capacity per container of 15+ double-sleeved cards, including Perfect Hards, or 20+ single-sleeved cards. The plastic packs have interlocking closures and a scratch-resistant coating. Publisher's product page

  • Amazon listing — Land Panorama Set, ten-pack
  • Reorder identifiers: ASIN B0C1NGBT7W; Cubeamajigs Reusable Gaming Packs — Land Panorama Set; artist Daarken; publisher Hit Point Press.
  • Draft quantity: an eight-player draft with three fifteen-card packs each needs twenty-four containers. Three of these ten-packs supply thirty, leaving six spares.

Find it again: browse Hit Point Press's Cubeamajigs collection for this set and other decorative designs, or search Cubeamajigs Land Panorama Daarken. Check the artwork and retail quantity on the replacement listing. The advertised “up to 400 cards” refers to the ten-pack's combined unsleeved capacity, not double-sleeved capacity per container.

Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes

Preferred product family. Clear folding boxes sold in ten-packs, with published internal dimensions. The 37S offers more room for tokens, references, and double sleeves.

Purchase link Internal dimensions Reorder identifier
30 sleeved / 30S, ten-pack 67 × 95.5 × 18.5 mm Variant 39339002167385
37 sleeved / 37S, ten-pack 67 × 95.5 × 22.5 mm Variant 21689594544217

The size labels are not guaranteed double-sleeved card counts. Our reviewed depth estimates are 25–27 double-sleeved cards for 30S and 31–33 for 37S, with no padding or removal allowance; see the comparison and its assumptions. Physical fit with the project's sleeves and stickers remains untested.

Find it again: manufacturer's product page or Burger Tokens. Search for Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes 30 sleeved or 37 sleeved. Recheck the chosen variant's stock and dispatch estimate when ordering.

APACALI clear snap-tight card shells

Alternative with estimated interior dimensions. The selected Amazon item is a twenty-four-pack of clear hinged cases, advertised as 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.82 inches with 1.2 mm walls.

  • Original Amazon listing
  • Short ASIN link
  • Reorder identifier: ASIN B09CH5VK1G; brand APACALI; 24-pack.

Treat the listed size as exterior. Subtracting the advertised walls estimates an interior of 66.18 × 94.12 × 18.43 mm, but the description also rounds the exterior depth to 0.8 inches. The calculator's 0.82-inch model gives 25–27 double-sleeved cards by depth. Width is a tighter concern: a 66 mm sleeve would have only 0.18 mm total clearance before molded corners or tolerances. Confirm fit before buying a full collection's supply.

Find it again: search for APACALI 24 Pack Card Deck Box B09CH5VK1G. The original URL contains “0.94” despite the selected item's 0.82-inch title; match the ASIN, pack count, and current dimensions when reordering. See the storage research for the dimension discrepancy.

Slim Jumpstart deck boxes from jman2c on eBay

Compact alternative for twenty-card packs; not selected for the twenty-five-card double-sleeved target. The seller advertises 20+ double-sleeved, 25+ single-sleeved, or 45+ unsleeved cards. No numerical interior dimensions or tested sleeve combination were supplied. These are opaque, latching boxes with interlocking sides and a card-removal slot.

  • eBay product listing
  • Seller's other listings
  • Reorder identifiers: item 227289326606; seller jman2c; search Jumpstart Deck Box Slim Deck Boxes Trading Card Game Cubes 20+ Cards.

On the review date, the listing offered new boxes in multiple colors and quantities of 1, 3, 15, 24, or 36, with more than ten available. That suggests a repeatable product listing rather than a one-off sale, though continued supply is not guaranteed. The seller had 99.6% positive feedback, but the sole feedback entry for this item was an automated delivery note, not a fit or durability review. These details and the capacity claim come from the listing and its seller description.

A matching Etsy listing from UsefulExtrusion had 4.3/5 across seven item reviews, including successful Jumpstart use and an insufficient-capacity report. The seller's response specifies twenty double-sleeved cards, with only one or two more potentially squeezed in. Its title, photos, and description match the eBay product, but the two seller accounts have not been explicitly linked. The UsefulExtrusion shop provides another route to investigate if the item disappears.

Assessment: worth considering for a small twenty-card packet after a sample fit check. It offers no established allowance for five additional double-sleeved cards, and long-term durability remains unverified.

Tools

The Sturdy Deck Press

MakerWorld model and print files by Nabes Teban. A 3D-printable press for removing trapped air from sleeved or double-sleeved cards, suggested for the Jumpstart preparation kit. Cards load from the side; the upper tray attaches to the screw, and an optional lower tray helps with removal.

  • Print profile: the designer's profile 2018145 specifies 0.2 mm layers, nine walls, and 33% infill. The original design uses ABS; the designer also recommends PETG in the comments. Check the current profile and assembly instructions for the chosen printer and material.
  • Small-packet fit: the designer confirms that the screw intentionally stops short of the base and suggests adding tokens, lands, or bulk cards when a stack is too short. An individual twenty- or twenty-five-card Jumpstart packet may need that additional stack height. Pressing does not establish a fixed final card thickness; measure the actual packet for the storage calculator.
  • Find it again: MakerWorld model 1884468, The STURDY DECK PRESS, creator Nabes Teban. The creator's profile provides a fallback if the model URL changes.

Card storage calculator

Open the calculator to work from either a card stack or a box. It supports single and double sleeves, separate compressed and post-play measurements, inserts, padding, removal clearance, and editable box dimensions. The Burger Tokens and APACALI presets connect these saved products to the sizing calculations.

Keeping the list useful

When a link disappears, search the saved product name and identifier, or return to the manufacturer or seller. Confirm dimensions and size variants again before treating a replacement listing as the same product. For a successful purchase, add the chosen size, actual measured interior, sleeve combination, and comfortable card count here so the next order can use a tested fit.
