Marvel Sets and Supporting Card Pools

Checked: October 19, 2026. This reference collects Marvel-branded Magic printings regardless of which product they came from, followed by other sets that could support Marvel-themed game-night boxes. It is a pool of possibilities, not a shopping list or a commitment to build one box from every set.

The released Marvel search currently reaches 21 Scryfall set codes. That includes six principal card pools, nine promotional categories, three token sets, and three memorabilia categories. It does not mean there have been 21 separate Marvel expansions. Shared codes such as SLD contain much more than Marvel, and one retail release can use several codes.

Finding Marvel cards across products

Scryfall's working art tag is art:marvel-comics. This catches Marvel artwork on reprints as well as cards named after Marvel characters. It is more useful than searching only names or assuming every Marvel release has its own exclusive code. Art tags are community maintained, so a newly added printing can be missing a tag.

Purpose Search
Browse Marvel cards for deck ideas Marvel artwork, one result per card
See alternate printings and art Marvel artwork, every printing
Include tokens and other extras Marvel artwork with extras and all printings
Reproduce this released-only snapshot Marvel printings dated on or before October 19, 2026
Find Marvel Secret Lair cards without unrelated drops Marvel artwork in SLD

The broad queries without a date cutoff can include previews. unique:prints intentionally repeats cards with different printings; omit it when choosing distinct cards for a singleton pool. include:extras exposes objects such as tokens and art cards that are not ordinary deck cards.

Principal released Marvel card pools

The names below are Scryfall's exact set names. Set links open the whole category; use the Marvel art tag when a category also contains unrelated material.

Code Set name Why it belongs in the search
SPM Marvel's Spider-Man Main Spider-Man expansion, released September 26, 2025.
SPE Marvel's Spider-Man Eternal Supplemental Spider-Man cards under a separate code, also September 26, 2025. Searching only SPM misses this pool.
MSH Marvel Super Heroes Main Marvel expansion, released June 26, 2026.
MSC Marvel Super Heroes Commander Includes the Marvel Commander and Jumpstart card pool. The name does not mean every MSC card came from a Commander deck. Released June 26, 2026.
MAR Marvel Universe Marvel Source Material reprints, including material associated with different Marvel releases. The category began September 26, 2025.
SLD Secret Lair Drop The shared Secret Lair category contains the early Marvel character drops and later Marvel releases. Filter it by Marvel artwork.

Wizards' Marvel Jumpstart themes and decklists explicitly identify new Jumpstart cards with MSC. The Marvel collecting guide is the companion product reference.

The first Marvel Secret Lair Superdrop went on sale November 4, 2024, before the Spider-Man expansion. It consisted of five character drops: Black Panther, Captain America, Iron Man, Wolverine, and Storm. Those drops share SLD; they are not five separate Scryfall set codes. The date of the overall SLD category is not the release date of its Marvel cards.

To browse the main dedicated pools plus Marvel Secret Lair cards, use:

(set:spm or set:spe or set:msh or set:msc or set:mar or (set:sld art:marvel-comics))

That is a practical deck-building search, not the complete promotional and memorabilia inventory below.

Tokens and memorabilia

Code Exact set name Use in a box
TSPM Marvel's Spider-Man Tokens Token components, including the 1/1 flying Robot.
TMSH Marvel Super Heroes Tokens Token components for the main Marvel release.
TMSC Marvel Super Heroes Commander Tokens Token components associated with MSC.
ASPM Marvel's Spider-Man Art Series Art cards, not ordinary playable Magic cards. Potential decorative dividers.

Tokens can be physical components of a Horde under its selected rules. That does not turn a token into an ordinary deck card in every format.

Other sets that can support Marvel themes

These are thematic suggestions. A setting can offer useful supporting cards without needing its own dedicated crossover box. In particular, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles and Transformers can supply occasional guests in a broader Marvel game.

Superheroes, city life, and crossover guests

Codes and exact names Useful material Fit to check
TMT Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles; TMC Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Eternal; PZA Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Source Material Other comic-book heroes, New York, street life, pizza, ninjas, and machines. TTMT Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Tokens supplies tokens. Named turtles and mutants are explicit crossover guests. Ordinary city props can blend more quietly.
BOT Transformers Named robot heroes and villains, often with vehicle faces. BOT is its own code even though the cards appeared alongside The Brothers' War. Named Autobots/Decepticons are not anonymous Ultron drones.
SNC Streets of New Capenna; NCC New Capenna Commander Urban streets, citizens, organized crime, businesses, and vehicles. Art Deco fantasy city; many inhabitants and objects are supernatural. Select individually for an ordinary modern-city joke.
MKM Murders at Karlov Manor; MKC Murders at Karlov Manor Commander Detectives, witnesses, evidence, and everyday objects. Ravnica is a fantasy city. Investigators may be nonhuman or magical even when their jobs sound ordinary.
NEO Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty; NEC Neon Dynasty Commander Futuristic urban technology, vehicles, and machines. Neon city imagery can fit a technological Marvel box; spirits, cybernetics, and samurai are more specific departures.
PIP Fallout; TPIP Fallout Tokens Actual Robot creatures, appliances, supplies, and retro technology. A post-apocalyptic franchise with recognizable robot models. A useful machine source, but not franchise-neutral by default.

See the separate everyday-life catalogue for individual mundane cards and clearly marked almost matches.

Generic machine sources

Codes and exact names What to browse Visual character
KLD Kaladesh; AER Aether Revolt Constructs, Servos, Thopters, inventors, and artifact support. Tokens: TKLD Kaladesh Tokens, TAER Aether Revolt Tokens. Ornate industrial inventions; often easier to cast as an inventor's machines than a spacecraft crew.
NEO Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Small artifact creatures such as Patchwork Automaton and Iron Apprentice. Tokens: TNEO Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Tokens. Futuristic machines in an urban setting. Both named examples are Constructs.
MRD Mirrodin; SOM Scars of Mirrodin; MBS Mirrodin Besieged Myr, Constructs, artifact creatures, and mechanical armies. A metallic fantasy world. Useful machine silhouettes, but some cards are visibly alien or Phyrexian.
EOE Edge of Eternities; EOC Edge of Eternities Commander Robots and artifact support already used in the owner's earlier robot Creature Feature deck. Tokens: TEOE Edge of Eternities Tokens. Explicit space imagery. Good for cosmic Marvel; individual art may feel less grounded in New York.

For machines across Magic's own settings, try:

(t:robot or t:construct or t:myr or t:servo or t:thopter) t:creature -is:ub

Open the machine search. This is a discovery query, not a list of cards with the Robot type or a pre-approved Horde list.

Actual Robot tokens

A token named Robot exists in several sizes. Pick the token profile the box's rules actually call for; these are not interchangeable rules objects.

Printing Characteristics Theme consideration
TTMT 10 — Robot 1/1 colorless artifact creature — Robot; no abilities. TMNT artwork.
TSPM 6 — Robot 1/1 colorless artifact creature — Robot with flying. Spider-Man artwork; flying changes the Horde's combat.
TEOE 10 — Robot 2/2 colorless artifact creature — Robot; no abilities. The existing space-themed option.
TPIP 16 — Robot 3/3 colorless artifact creature — Robot; no abilities. Fallout artwork; a simple rules profile.
T40K 23 — Robot 4/4 colorless artifact creature — Robot; cannot block. Warhammer 40,000 artwork and a distinct restriction.

Browse every Robot token printing. Cards named Servo or Thopter can also look robotic, but they have their own types and characteristics.

Using this research in boxes

Choose the game rules first, then build a thematic card pool that follows them. Creature Feature counts actual creature types: Robot, Construct, Myr, Servo, and Thopter are not synonyms. A supporting creature that looks like a robot does not automatically meet a Robot requirement. See the complete Creature Feature rules.

A Horde can mix mechanical-looking creature types when its selected rules allow it. Check targeting, optional abilities, repeated activations, and infinite-mana interactions before adding complex cards. Any special automation procedure belongs explicitly in that box's rules. Do not assume a thematic card can run itself.

For an Age of Ultron idea, anonymous machines can be more coherent than importing another villain's distinctive army. The verified card Ultron, Machine Overlord is a Robot Villain and gives other Robots and Constructs +2/+2. That is a specific card option, not a requirement to buy Ultron or make every Marvel card fit that box.

The project's preference is variety and usually one copy of each nonbasic card, with duplicates justified by a real play requirement. A themed box need not consume every spare card. Several smaller boxes, a singleton cube, and reusable Jumpstart packets can use different parts of these pools.

Sources and maintenance

  • Set names and codes: Scryfall set directory and set metadata API, checked October 19, 2026.
  • Card names, types, token profiles, and searches: Scryfall, queried through the project's Scryfall MCP server.
  • Product context: Wizards' 2024 Marvel Secret Lair announcement, Marvel collecting guide, and Marvel Jumpstart themes.

The 21-code inventory was obtained by searching art:marvel-comics date<=2026-10-19 include:extras unique:prints, then excluding already found set codes until no results remained. A cross-check of the dedicated SPM, SPE, MSH, MSC, and MAR pools found no released cards missing that art tag at the time. This is a dated best-effort catalogue, not a promise that future printings will be tagged immediately.

When updating it, repeat the unrestricted Marvel-art search, inspect new set codes, check individual printing dates in shared promotional/Secret Lair categories, and keep upcoming material separate from released cards. Preserve this distinction rather than treating a set category's start date as every card's release date.
