How to use this calculator

Choose a thickness method, enter the number of cards, and set the box's usable interior dimensions. The calculator estimates stack depth, remaining space, and total card capacity. Include title cards, tokens, references, and padding in the contents budget. Different stock or sleeving needs its own thickness estimate. Width and height must fit as well as depth.

Measured stacks are the most useful input. Measure a known number of cards with the actual sleeves and tracking stickers, then divide stack depth by card count. Record compressed and uncompressed or post-play measurements separately. Measure the thickest point: aligned stickers can produce a local bulge. Single and double sleeving describe your sample; they do not establish its thickness automatically. There is no verified universal compression factor.

The published presets use practical measurements from Double Sleeved's double-sleeving guide: 100 cards with Gamegenic Inner and Matte Prime sleeves measured 68 mm; Dragon Shield Perfect Fit and Matte measured 72 mm. These become 0.68 and 0.72 mm per card, respectively. They are different sleeve combinations, not compressed and uncompressed states of one stack. The guide does not document a reproducible compression protocol or test this project's stickers.

Use a custom thickness range for other evidence. The optional layer estimate adds bare-card thickness, both outer-sleeve faces, and both inner-sleeve faces when double sleeving. Its 0.30 mm bare card is an assumption. Dragon Shield describes most standard sleeves as 0.12 mm per layer, not per assembled sleeve. The guide measured Perfect Fit inner layers at 0.06 mm. Film arithmetic does not include trapped air or guarantee a finished stack measurement.

Product dimensions and interpretation

Find purchase links, exact variants, and reorder identifiers in Useful Products & Tools.

Burger Tokens publishes interior dimensions for 30S and 37S. APACALI's preset instead estimates the interior by subtracting two advertised wall thicknesses from its exterior dimensions; its listing is inconsistent. Gamegenic's double-sleeving pack supplies the 66 × 91 mm outer-sleeve footprint example. Replace it for another sleeve model.

Capacity rounds down after subtracting any handling allowance. Zero allowance shows a mathematical limit, not a comfortable-fit guarantee. The Jumpstart storage research records the product comparison, dimension uncertainty, and historical Google handover. Its original thickness estimates are retained for provenance and superseded for purchase decisions.
