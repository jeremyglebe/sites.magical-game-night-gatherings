# New York Animal Invasion

[All boxed sets](./) · [Horde format family](../formats/horde/)

::: warning Future concept — not a playable box
New York is invaded by ordinary animals, and ordinary people face the invasion. This is the planned first **Horde** box; the first box being designed overall is **Zombies vs Plants: Jumpstart**. Horde design and playtesting are deferred. No base ruleset, decks, or complete play procedure has been selected for this concept.
:::

## Premise

Make mundane animals and ordinary New Yorkers the focus of a cooperative Horde game. The animal side supplies the invasion; the people's side represents residents dealing with it. Everyday places and activities establish the New York setting.

This is a theme direction, not a change to any card's name, type, abilities, or rules text. The existing research offers candidates, not an approved card pool or a decklist.

## Base ruleset

**Not yet selected.** The [Horde family page](../formats/horde/) keeps the available versions separate. Their setup, enemy-deck procedures, targeting, and victory conditions differ. Select one complete version before deciding whether this box needs additions or replacements.

## Research and origin

The premise is the owner's box concept. It is not a claim that an existing published Horde format uses this theme or these undecided rules.

The existing [Everyday Life and Ordinary New York research](../research/everyday-life.md) provides starting material:

- [Pets, pests, and ordinary wildlife](../research/everyday-life/animals.md) separates close everyday-animal matches from setting stretches.
- [People and ordinary jobs](../research/everyday-life/people.md) records civilian candidates and their actual card types.
- [Places and everyday activities](../research/everyday-life/places-and-activities.md) supplies setting and noncreature-card possibilities.
- [Ordinary-animal tokens](../research/everyday-life/tokens.md) distinguishes token profiles and explains why a shared animal name does not make tokens interchangeable.

These are reference notes for later selection. They do not prescribe the box's decks or override the eventual base ruleset.
