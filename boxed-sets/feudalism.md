## Concept

Feudalism is a hidden-role game-night box built around the monarch mechanic. Players can fight over the crown, but power returns to the King until the group breaks that structure. A Renegade-style role is intended to win by properly dethroning the King; what qualifies as dethroning still needs a rule.

## Recorded additional rules

### F1. Starting monarchy

The player with the King role starts the game as the monarch.

### F2. The crown returns

At the start of the King's turn, if the King is not the monarch, the King becomes the monarch.

The original concept says “at the start of the King's turn.” Before playtesting, decide whether this happens before untapping or as a triggered ability at the beginning of upkeep. Those timings behave differently; no timing has been chosen here.

### F3. Ordinary monarchy

The **King role** and **being the monarch** are different things. The recorded rule changes who has the monarch designation; it does not say that taking the monarch designation changes a hidden role.

Unless the completed box explicitly replaces them, the ordinary monarch rules apply:

- Exactly one player is the monarch once the designation has entered the game. A new monarch replaces the previous one.
- At the beginning of the monarch's end step, the monarch's inherent ability triggers and that player draws a card when it resolves.
- When a creature deals combat damage to the monarch, an inherent ability triggers; when it resolves, that creature's controller becomes the monarch.
- If the monarch leaves the game, the active player becomes the monarch. If the active player is leaving too, the next player in turn order becomes the monarch.

These are ordinary monarch rules, checked against the [official rulings for Palace Jailer](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/140/palace-jailer). They do not define Feudalism's dethroning victory condition.

## Deck and component requirements

The recorded deck requirements are:

- Every deck features monarch cards or cards that care about becoming the monarch.
- Every deck works with any role it might receive.
- The decks encourage contesting the crown while leaving the King vulnerable to pressure.

Deck count, decklists, supported player counts, role cards, and starting-life aids remain to be specified. Include a monarch marker and a reminder for F2 when preparing the first playtest kit.
