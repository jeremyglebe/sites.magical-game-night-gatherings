## Turns and combat

The archenemy takes the first turn and draws during their first draw step. Then the hero team takes a shared turn. Alternate archenemy turns and team turns.

During a team turn, heroes move through the phases and steps together. Each untaps their own permanents, takes their own draw, and may use their own land play. They make their own decisions, pay their own costs, and keep their own hands, libraries, mana, and permanents. Being teammates does not let them spend one another's mana or cards.

The heroes declare their attacks together against the archenemy or legal objects that can be attacked. When the archenemy attacks, declare which hero or attackable object each creature attacks. Heroes declare blockers together and can use their creatures to defend a teammate or that teammate's planeswalker. They cannot attack one another.

Use the shared-team-turn rules for priority, simultaneous decisions, and combat. Individual card restrictions still apply; being on a team does not allow an otherwise illegal target or block.
