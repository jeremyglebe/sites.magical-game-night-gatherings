## The Horde's turn

After the setup turns, alternate a Horde turn with a shared survivor turn.

1. Reveal the top card of the Horde library.
2. If it is a Zombie token, continue revealing.
3. Stop at the first non-token card, or when the library runs out.
4. Cast the revealed tokens, then cast the revealed non-token card last if there is one.
5. Proceed to combat. Horde Zombies have haste and must attack if able.

The Horde's spells use the stack and players can respond, including countering them. Having infinite mana does not mean they bypass the stack.

The Horde attacks the survivor team. Survivors can block together for the team. Horde Zombies cannot block, so attacking the Horde does not require getting past its Zombies.

## Horde mana, choices, and cards

The Horde has unlimited mana for costs. Mana taxes such as Propaganda or Mana Leak do not stop it by requiring a payment it cannot afford. Non-mana costs still require whatever the card instructs.

Build the Horde to minimize choices. When an effect nevertheless requires a choice, make it as randomly as possible among legal options.

A permanent returned to the Horde's hand is cast again during its next main phase. The Horde starts with no hand; that does not erase the original rule for cards returned to its hand.

When a card interaction does not function in this automated format, the original source asks the group to choose a fair handling or cycle the problematic card. Agree the exact replacement procedure if using that escape hatch; the source does not define its cycling cost or timing.
